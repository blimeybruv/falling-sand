Falling Sand

Controls

Left-drag / fingerDraw the selected material
Right-drag / two fingersErase
Number and letter keysPick a material (the key is shown on each button)
EEraser
[ and ]Smaller / bigger brush
Shift+B, L, R, O, XDraw with the brush, a line, a box, a circle, or the replace brush (the buttons beside the material tabs). Drag a line, box or circle out and let go; the brush size sets how thick it is. Replace swaps the material you start on for the selected one
Shift+FFilled or outlined boxes and circles
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo (also in the Scene menu)
SpacePause / play
.Step one frame while paused
THeat view: show temperatures
Edges buttonWalls (things pile up against the edges) or open (they fall off the bottom, flow out of the sides and rise out of the top)
Speaker buttonSound on or off: wind, fire, explosions, thunder, rain and more, and a chime for each discovery
Scene menuSave, load, download, open, or copy a link to your scene
JDiscovery journal
? or HShow / hide this panel

Discovery mode: you start with a few materials. Make new ones happen in the sandbox (fire meets water…) to unlock them. Locked "?" buttons give a hint, and the journal keeps track. Switch to Sandbox mode (on a phone, open the extra controls with the downward arrow) to have everything.

Tips: plant seeds near water. Pour oil onto water and set it alight. Drop nitroglycerin from a height. Lay a gunpowder fuse. Pour lava into a pond. Heat a stone pot of water from below, and watch it in heat view.
