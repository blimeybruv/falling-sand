EEraser
[ and ]Smaller / bigger brush
SpacePause / play
.Step one frame while paused

Scene menuSave, load, download, open, or copy a link to your scene
JDiscovery journal
? or HShow / hide this panel

Discovery mode: you start with a few materials. Make new ones happen in the sandbox (fire meets water…) to unlock them. Locked "?" buttons give a hint, and the journal keeps track. Switch to Sandbox mode (under ⋯ on a phone) to have everything.

Tips: plant seeds near water. Pour oil onto water and set it alight. Drop nitroglycerin from a height. Lay a gunpowder fuse. Pour lava into a pond. Heat a stone pot of water from below, and watch it in heat view.
